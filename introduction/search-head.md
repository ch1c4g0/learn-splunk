

<p><h1>Splunk Search Head,</h1></p>

<p>This is the place within search and reporting, here users can search the indexed logs.</p>

<p>Searching logs is done using SPL (Search Processing Language) which is a powerful language for searching indexed data.</p>

<p>Data requests(searches), are forwarded to the indexer and relevant evnets are returned in field-value pairs.</p>

<p>You can also transform results into presentable tables and visualizations such as pie, bar, and columns.</p>
